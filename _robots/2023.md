Charged Up is the game for the 2023 FIRST Robotics Competition. The game is themed around transportation and involves two alliances consisting of three teams each competing to perform various tasks, including picking up cubes and cones and transporting them into high, medium, and low nodes. There is also a balancing mechanic within the game, called the Charge Station. Robots must balance onto the Charge Station to earn points.
