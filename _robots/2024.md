Crescendo is the game for the 2024 FIRST Robotics Competition. The game is themed around music and involves two alliances consisting of three teams each competing to perform various tasks, including picking up "notes" and shooting them into the "speaker". There is also a hanging mechanic within the game, called the Stage. Robots must hang onto the Stage via chain to earn points, with an extra bonus of trap points and microphone boost.
