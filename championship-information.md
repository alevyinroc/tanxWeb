## We need your help to go to Houston!
{: .title-xl}

We are the Canandaigua FIRST Robotics Team - Team 3003 / TAN[X], and we're going to Houston! Every year we design, build, program, and operate an industrial-sized robot that plays a complicated field game, while also managing our own fundraising, community outreach, and unique team brand.
{: .lead}

On March 18, 2023, in alliance with Teams 1591, 3015, and 6868 we won the FIRST Robotics Finger Lakes Regional in Rochester NY! This qualifies us to compete with over 600 teams from around the world at the FIRST Championships in Houston, TX, April 19-22.
{: .lead}

This accomplishment adds approximately $35,000 to our annual budget. In order to keep travel costs low for team members, and volunteer mentors, we are asking for your help. Please consider a gift of any size to help us take Icarus the robot and our team members to Houston!
{: .lead}
