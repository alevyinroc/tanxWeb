## **Why Donate?**

Robotics builds a solid foundation in engineering and technology and gives a real-life perspective on math and science. Team 3003 is a robotics team from Canandaigua, NY, comprised of over 25 high school students. We participate in FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology), a worldwide robotics competition with thousands of teams and thousands of students from all over the world. This competition strives to inspire young people to be leaders in the technological and scientific world. Our robotics program encourages innovation and fosters well-rounded life skills, including self-confidence, communication, and leadership. As a non-profit school club running a high-budget program, we rely on sponsor donations, in-kind and cash, to continue our work.
{: .lead .text-center}

![Average annual cost: $37,000. Transportation and lodging 46%, event fees 27%, robot parts and tools 24%, marketing 3%. All donations are tax deductible!]({{ '/assets/images/sponsorship/budget-chart.png' | relative_url }}){: .img-center}

The Canandaigua robotics team has an annual operating budget of approximately $37,000. The many parts we use to build our robots, materials needed to market our team, and costs for travel, food, and registration fees for competitions add up quickly. We turn to family, friends, individual supporters, and corporate sponsors to help us raise the money we need to have a team year to year.
{: .lead .text-center}

Your sponsorship will cover the tremendous costs of building an industrial-grade robot and competing in regional and national competitions for both the Middle School and High School robotics teams. We would love to come and do a demonstration for your company so that we can tell you and show you what our team is all about.
{: .lead .text-center}
